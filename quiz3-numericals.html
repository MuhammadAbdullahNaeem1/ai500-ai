The notes' back questions that ask for a number are Q19 (missingness rate), Q38 (mean vs median of {2,3,3,4,100}\{2,3,3,4,100\}{2,3,3,4,100}) and Q42 (80th percentile, Type 7 vs Type 6). Worked example: Notes Q38: {2,3,3,4,100}\{2,3,3,4,100\}{2,3,3,4,100}

xˉ=2+3+3+4+1005=1125=22.4\bar x=\dfrac{2+3+3+4+100}{5}=\dfrac{112}{5}=22.4xˉ=52+3+3+4+100​=5112​=22.4, and x~=3\tilde x=3x~=3 (the 3rd of 5 ordered values).
