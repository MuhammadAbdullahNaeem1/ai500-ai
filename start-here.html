Start here

How to use this site in the five days before Quiz 2 (Saturday 24 October), and before the mid.

#If Quiz 2 is in a few days

  1. Day 1 — Module 2 notes, Part A (2.1–2.3): data as a constructed record, the eight data sources, the six quality dimensions. At every ★ Remember line, cover the page and say it aloud.
  2. Day 2 — Parts B and C (curation, cleaning, leakage; documentation, governance, case studies).
  3. Day 3 — answers Q1–100 (1–50, 51–100). Answer aloud before opening each box; mark the ones you missed.
  4. Day 4 — scenarios (S1–S5) and two reflections (R1–R5); then Mock Quiz 2A under a 60-minute timer, marked with the rubric.
  5. Day 5 — Mock 2B, Mock 2C, the Quiz 2 guide one-liners, and your A2 Part A answers.

#How the pages are built

  • Notes follow the order of the module notes (section numbers in headings). Boxes: blue = definition, green = worked example from the notes, red = common trap, yellow = how it is examined, rust ★ Remember closes every section. Tables and figures from the notes appear as snapshots — tap to enlarge.
  • Practice answers cover every end-of-module question (Module 2: Q1–100, S1–S5, R1–R5; Module 1: Q1–100, S1–S5, R1–R5, A1–A7), using the notes' definitions and examples. Use Show all solutions at the top to reveal everything for revision.
  • Mock quizzes copy Quiz 1 exactly: definitions (9) · short conceptual (12) · scenario (21) · identify-the-problems (8) = 50 points in 60 minutes, with rubric-style marking.

#Sources used

The 2026 Module 1 and Module 2 notes (Dr. Hassan Mohy-ud-Din), the syllabus, the Quiz 1 solution scheme, Assignments 1 and 2 with your submissions, the Module 2 computational tutorial, and your Quiz 1 preparation guide. Material from the previous offering of AI 500 is on the resources page.
