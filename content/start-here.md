# Start here

How to use this site in the five days before Quiz 2 (Saturday 24 October), and before the mid.

## If Quiz 2 is in a few days

1. **Day 1 — Module 2 notes, Part A** ([2.1–2.3](m2a-notes.html)): data as a constructed record, the eight data sources, the six quality dimensions. At every ★ Remember line, cover the page and say it aloud.
2. **Day 2 — Parts B and C** ([curation, cleaning, leakage](m2b-notes.html); [documentation, governance, case studies](m2c-notes.html)).
3. **Day 3 — answers Q1–100** ([1–50](m2-answers-1.html), [51–100](m2-answers-2.html)). Answer aloud before opening each box; mark the ones you missed.
4. **Day 4 — scenarios** ([S1–S5](m2-scenarios.html)) and two reflections ([R1–R5](m2-reflections.html)); then **[Mock Quiz 2A](quiz2-mock-a.html)** under a 60-minute timer, marked with the rubric.
5. **Day 5 — [Mock 2B](quiz2-mock-b.html), [Mock 2C](quiz2-mock-c.html)**, the [Quiz 2 guide](quiz2-guide.html) one-liners, and your [A2 Part A answers](a2-missedcare.html).

## How the pages are built

- **Notes** follow the order of the module notes (section numbers in headings). Boxes: blue = definition, green = worked example from the notes, red = common trap, yellow = how it is examined, rust **★ Remember** closes every section. Tables and figures from the notes appear as snapshots — tap to enlarge.
- **Practice answers** cover every end-of-module question (Module 2: Q1–100, S1–S5, R1–R5; Module 1: Q1–100, S1–S5, R1–R5, A1–A7), using the notes' definitions and examples. Use **Show all solutions** at the top to reveal everything for revision.
- **Mock quizzes** copy Quiz 1 exactly: definitions (9) · short conceptual (12) · scenario (21) · identify-the-problems (8) = 50 points in 60 minutes, with rubric-style marking.
